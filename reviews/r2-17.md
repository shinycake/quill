## Defects

1. 05-062, `src/state/fetch.rs:296` (`succeed`) and `:302` (`fail`). Only the sticker lists, greeting stickers, emoji icon lists and live story extras were moved to `answered`/`failed`. About 290 other callers still take answers by purpose only and are frozen in `scripts/fetch-unmatched-baseline.txt` (126 lines). The decision doc gives "each needs its sender to record its request" as the reason, which is the banned "touches many sites". Scenario: any of those fetches is refreshed while an older request is in flight. The older answer lands on the newer ask. Fix: migrate them, or keep the entry open and list the files. A freezing baseline is not a fix.
2. 04-046, `src/pull_next/mod.rs:311`. `Pull::holding: bool` is still there. It is set at `:671` from `movement()` and read at `:808`. It is a stored copy of the edge stage, written only in `handle()`. The entry names it. Fix: derive it from `PullMachine::edge` (`self.edge == Edge::Held`) in `push_indicator` and delete the field.
3. `src/pull_next/mod.rs:245`. `Step::started` stays in `scripts/implicit-state-baseline.txt`, and the file was touched. It is a per-call output event and not a stage. Either move it to `implicit-state-allow.txt` with that reason or make it an enum on `Step`. The file-complete rule gives no third option.
4. `src/state/call_types.rs` (`ActiveGroupCall`). The bundle's own second read found `stream_mode` with `stream`, and `is_joined` with `rejoin`, and left both. Its reason is "the engine's `GroupCallMode`, which this bundle does not own". That is the banned "owned by another bundle". The file must end with zero findings. Fix them or prove them independent by their writers. `loaded_all_participants` also keeps a baseline line in the same file. The allow file has only the combined key `loaded_all_participants+screen_share_error`.
5. 05-009, `src/state/call_types.rs`. `ActiveCall::ready` and `transport` are kept beside `state`. The skip says `ready` is kept through a hang-up. That does not make it independent of the phase. `ready` is still written at the Ready transition and read beside `phase()`. The entry names `ready` and `transport`. Fix: fold `ready` into the call's phase payload, or prove by writers and readers that no combination is invalid.

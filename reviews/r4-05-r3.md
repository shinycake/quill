3. **src/connect/calls/mod.rs:688 and src/state/call_transport.rs:139: the reconnect deadline comes from the wall clock, the test depends on it, and nothing wakes the loop at the deadline.**
   - Both sites call `CallTransport::reconnecting(link, Instant::now())`, although `request_tick(now)` already has the driver's `now`.
   - The test `a_silent_retry_runs_out_at_its_deadline_and_spends_the_budget` (connect/tests/calls/mod.rs:426-460) takes `start = Instant::now()`. Each later deadline is the real time plus 20 s, not the test's `now` plus 20 s, so the test passes only while less than about 20 s of real time goes by. That is a wall-clock read in a test.
   - `ConnectDriver::next_deadline` (flood_retry.rs:187-245) does not include the deadline. The poll loop sets its sleep from `next_deadline` (ui/notifications/mod.rs:213). A silent retry therefore ends only when something else happens to wake the loop.
   - Fix:
     - Pass `now: Instant` into `pump_call_engine_reports(now)`, `apply_transport_report(state, now)` and `CallTransport::reported(state, link, now)`, from `request_tick`'s `now`. Then `Instant::now()` goes from both sites.
     - Add `CallTransport::reconnect_deadline() -> Option<Instant>`, and add `active_call.transport.reconnect_deadline().map(|d| d.saturating_duration_since(now))` to `next_deadline`.
   - Tests:
     - Rewrite the test with a fixed `t0` passed to `request_tick(t0)` / `expire_call_reconnect`, and no `Instant::now()` in the test.
     - Add a test that `next_deadline(t0, ms)` is `Some(CALL_RECONNECT_WINDOW)` right after a retry starts at `t0`.

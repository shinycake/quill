# Review of claude/sweep-r2-10: FIXED by the reviewer, merged into claude/sweep-r2-followup
## Confirmed defects (all fixed on the branch)
1. src/paid_reaction.rs (04-011): a refused commit became `PaidStage::Stuck`, on the claim that TDLib still holds the Stars. It does not: `MessageReactions::drop_pending_paid_reactions` zeroes the pending amount before `sendPaidReaction` goes out, and `SendPaidReactionQuery::on_error` unlocks the Stars (MessageReaction.cpp, MessagesManager.cpp). So the stuck amount stayed in `total_pending`, could raise "Not enough Stars" on other messages with nothing shown, and was sent again with the next tap. Fix: `refused(request)` releases a refused commit; only a refused undo (`removePendingPaidMessageReactions`, where TDLib does still hold them) stays `Stuck` and joins the next tap of its message. Tests rewritten on both levels.
2. src/psa_about.rs (04-045): a failed write of the seen flags went back to `Unsaved`, and `sync_save` runs on every pump, so a folder that cannot be written was written to on every pass. Fix: `Save::Failed`, which the pump does not restart; the next new flag saves everything. Test `a_failed_write_waits_for_the_next_flag`.
3. src/search_filters.rs: `in_scope` moved any tab to Chats, so a hashtag search opened from Photos or Channels lost its tab. It keeps the tab when the scope does not change.

## Must fix (S3, confirmed)

- **src/state/session_date_jump.rs:256-273 (`fail_date_jump`), with src/connect/search/chat_search.rs:493 and src/ui/jump_date.rs:240-242.** A failed history date lookup is never shown.
  - Cause: `jump_to_date` takes `history_calendar` before it sends, so the box closes at once. `fail_date_jump` stores `JumpLookup::NotFound(DateJumpNote::Failed)`. Its only reader is `jump_date_body`, which draws only while a calendar is open. Opening the box again runs `clear_date_jump_note()` first (session_date_jump.rs:116), which wipes the note.
  - Trigger: in a chat, choose Jump to date and pick a day that is not a calendar day. `getChatMessageByDate` answers an error other than 404 (420 FLOOD_WAIT, 500).
  - Effect: "Jumping to date…" and then nothing, with no error. The dead note sits in `jump_lookups` until the next open.
  - Fix: on a non-404 error, put a status note in the asking window with `set_note_for(pending.asked_in, ..)`, as the refused-send path in `jump_date_pick` (jump_date.rs:66-70) does, and do not store a `NotFound` that nothing draws. The other way is to keep the box open while `Asking` and take it only when the lookup resolves. Add a session test: an error answer gives the window a note.

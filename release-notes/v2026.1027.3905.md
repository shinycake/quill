### Agent access

AI agents on your computer, such as Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Cursor, can now use your Telegram account through the Quill you already have running. It's off until you turn it on in Settings > Agent access.

- Agents start with Saved Messages and bots. They have to ask for any other chat, and you allow it in Quill. Your contacts stay hidden until you share them.
- The Agent access page says in plain words what agents can do right now. Every tool is listed as available, asks first or off, with the reason. Account changes, money and Stars, and changes beyond one chat always wait for your click. Agents can never sign you out or delete your account.
- Activity shows what each agent did, with a link to the message and Undo for its sends. Revoke disconnects one agent, and turning off Allow agents keeps them all out.
- If you let them, agents can click and type in Quill's window, only in the chats they're allowed. A glow sweeps around the window while one is driving, and pressing a key or moving the mouse hands control back to you.
- Each app gets one line to copy to connect it. Agents that don't use MCP can use the `quill agent` command line, and Claude Code can install a ready-made Quill skill.

### Telegram Premium

- AI compose, with your own writing styles.
- Video profile pictures play beside messages and in the chat list.
- Stealth Mode for stories.
- Birthday gifts, sent from the suggestion or a recipient list.
- Rich message drafts that sync between your devices.
- Each Premium upsell opens on its own feature preview.
- Limit boxes show the limits Telegram reports.
- Read times, Premium privacy options, and a wallpaper for both sides of a chat.
- All chats can move among your folder tabs.

### Also new

- Accept or reject requests to turn on sharing in a chat.
- The chat menu's Translate brings back a hidden translate bar.
- Captionless photos and albums no longer sit in a bubble.
- Message effects play once per message, in chat windows too.
- Reactions stop at your per-user limit.
- Voice and video notes are blocked to people who don't accept them.
- New chats can be hidden into the archive.
- The "Updated to" banner shows only once.
- macOS releases are now signed with a stable identity, so the keychain stops asking for access after each update.

Quill is an unofficial Telegram client and still in beta.

### New

- Communities from Telegram's latest update. You can add a chat to a community (with join requests and visibility), remove one, review pending requests, show the whole community as one chat in the list, set its notifications and ban members.
- Message widgets run in the chat. A bot's widget draws live inside its message, and widgets stop when they scroll out of view or their window closes.
- Quill now runs on TDLib 1.8.70, with 24 library updates and newer Linux screen sharing support.
- The font list in Settings > Appearance shows each font name in its own typeface.

### Fixes

- The "Proxy settings" link on the sign-in screen opens the proxy list. Before, clicking it did nothing until you signed in.
- A second incoming call shows on the lock screen with Decline and End & answer, and it rings. Before, it was invisible and silent.
- Business settings wait for your saved info to load. Before, an edit made too early could save defaults over your real greeting or hours.
- Clicking a notification on Windows no longer crashes Quill.
- A story reply you were typing can no longer be sent to the next person's story.
- Turning off the passcode and pressing Esc can no longer leave Quill locked with no passcode set.
- Logging out from the lock screen can no longer unlock Quill when the wipe can't start. Quill stays locked and says why.
- The date at the top of a chat is drawn once instead of twice.

### Chat windows

- Boxes, links and toasts open in the window that asked for them, not always in the main window.
- Inline bot results, the pinned bar, Instant View, spellcheck, middle-click autoscroll, the GIF tab, AI compose and media auto-download work in chat windows the same as in the main window.
- A wrong passcode typed in a chat window clears that window's field, and unlocking clears every window's field.

### Lock screen

- While Quill is locked, every window shows only the lock screen. Boxes, menus and tooltips come back in their own window after you unlock.
- A call can still be answered from the lock screen, including one on another account. Notifications treat a locked Quill as away.

### Reliability

- Quill went through nine more review rounds for hidden states that can get stuck, with about 370 fixes. Most were answers that landed on the wrong chat or window, loading spinners that never ended after an error, and screens that kept data from a previous account.
- Agent permissions you allow stay in effect, and queued agent writes stop when you change a chat's grant.
- Custom emoji keep loading through long rate limits instead of giving up.
- A profile's photo gallery refreshes when the photo changes.
- Approving or dismissing all join requests no longer brings handled requests back, and one failed approve no longer wipes the list.
- Switching topics while editing a message keeps your edit instead of sending the text as a new message.
- The admin rights editor loads fresh rights, so it can't undo another admin's change.
- Clips with no sound no longer open the audio device every frame on Linux and Windows.

### Not yet tested on a live account

- Communities, message widgets and most of the fixes above were checked with tests and offline demos, not a live account. Redial and the lock-screen call answer were not tried with two real call clients.

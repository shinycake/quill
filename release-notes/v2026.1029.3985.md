### Calls run on Telegram's own engine

- Voice and video calls, group calls, screen sharing and live streams now run on tgcalls, the call library Telegram Desktop uses, on macOS, Linux and Windows. It replaces ntgcalls. Please report any call that misbehaves.
- **Conference calls.** Telegram's end-to-end encrypted group calls now work. The encryption emoji show under the call's title, and the Add People box searches your contacts.
- Calls show the other person's low battery and the connection's signal bars, as Telegram Desktop does.
- In group calls, everyone's speaking level drives their row, and Quill tells you when you talk while muted.

### Emoji suggestions

- Typing `:` and a word suggests emoji from Telegram's emoji keywords, so `:happy` finds 😊. Recently used emoji come first.
- Words in the message box that have an emoji, like "pizza" or "cake", get a soft highlight. Point at one to swap it for its emoji. Turn it off in Settings > Chat > Emoji ("Highlight words with emoji"). Lookups stay on your device.
- Sticker suggestions go away after you send the emoji they were for.

### Also new

- macOS shortcuts work as they should again: Cmd+H hides Quill, Cmd+Opt+H hides other apps, and the Ctrl text keys (Ctrl+K, Ctrl+F and the rest) edit text instead of triggering Quill commands. The app menu has Services and Show All, and shows Settings (Cmd+,) from launch.
- On Linux and Windows, Quill no longer binds Super-key chords that belong to the desktop.
- Reordering pinned chats is easier to follow: the row lifts off as a card and a dashed outline marks where it will land.
- Updated GPUI Kit (0.8.1), GPUI (0.3.10) and other dependencies.

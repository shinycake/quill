Quill is an unofficial Telegram client and still in beta.

### New

- Automatic media download now lives in Data and Storage, set up like Telegram Desktop's (#1453). One switch turns all automatic downloads off. Under it, private chats, groups and channels each have their own switch and a box where you pick which media to fetch, set size limits for downloads and autoplay, and add chats that always or never download. Your old choices carry over.
- Picked files open a send dialog with every option in view: album or separate messages, quality, caption above or below, self-destruct and spoilers. Paste or drop more files into it, and edit photos and videos from their tiles.
- Bots that allow threads show a New Thread card, and a message sent from a bot's All view starts its own thread.
- A bot's "What can this bot do?" card shows the bot's photo or plays its animation, and an empty bot chat shows only that card.
- Rename an account in the Accounts dialog, and switch accounts with Alt+1 to Alt+6 on Windows and Linux, or Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+6 on macOS (#1442).
- Albums show their reactions, headers and replies bar, and service messages such as pins and joins show their reactions under the notice.
- The media viewer keeps its controls clear of the Dock or taskbar, and you can drag the video controls out of the way. Drop them near their spot, or double-click, and they snap home.
- Submenus open to the other side when there's no room, as in Telegram Desktop.

### Fixes

- The old Mobile data, Wi-Fi and Roaming rows are gone. They only applied to phones and often said "unavailable". "Use less data for calls" stays on the Calls page.
- A widget fills its bubble when nothing follows it. The grey strip under it is gone.
- The emoji strip offered for a one-word message closes after you send.
- Bot keyboards and inline buttons match Telegram Desktop more closely.

### Reliability

- Two full review rounds for hidden states that can get stuck, covering payments, gifts, Star reactions, the passcode, calls, media and settings. Every finding was fixed or explained, and new CI checks catch the same shapes when they are written.
- gpui-kit 0.9.1 and GPUI 0.3.12.

### Not yet tested on a live account

- The new download settings, bot threads and account switching were checked with tests and offline demos, not on a live account.

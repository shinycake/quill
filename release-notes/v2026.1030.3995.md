### Quill gives memory back

After 40 minutes of ordinary use, Quill 2026.1029 could sit at 500 MB or more on a Mac. Most of that came from one leak in animated stickers. The rest was pictures and glyphs that stayed in memory after nothing showed them any more.

- When an animated sticker's next frame wasn't ready in time, Quill showed an earlier one and then never let it go, in memory or on the GPU. On a 120 Hz Mac, a chat full of stickers could add about 20 MB a second, and leaving the chat gave none of it back. Frames now go as soon as they stop showing, and stickers animate as before.
- Rounded video tiles used a corner mask the size of the whole tile, and each new tile or window size made another. They now share four small corner masks.
- Pictures Quill makes itself, like a rotated photo, an edited photo's preview, a video cover or a sign-in QR code, leave the GPU when they're replaced or closed.
- After a moment of idle, Quill lets go of wallpaper pictures for window sizes you've left, emoji the screen no longer shows, and pictures from sticker packs opened in a dialog. One trip through the emoji panel used to keep about 16 MB on the GPU until you quit.
- While Quill is in the background, photos of chats you've left are released. While it's in front, the photos it keeps for scrolling back give up their GPU copy at idle and keep their decoded picture, so they still show at once when you scroll back.
- Replied-to messages, pinned lists and message translations are released once a chat's history is dropped, and a rotated photo goes when the viewer closes.

Measured on an M1 Pro Mac with Quill's demo data, 2026-10-29 and 30:

- A chat of animated stickers and the GIF and sticker tabs grew Quill by more than 1 GB in a minute. With this release, the same run leaves it within a few MB of where it started.
- Browsing photos, stickers, the emoji panel and several chats, then idling for 90 seconds, left Quill about 50 MB above a fresh launch with only the sticker fix. With the rest of this release it's about 23 MB.

### Also fixed

- In calls on stereo speakers, or on headsets that don't run at 48 kHz, the other person sounded sped up and high-pitched, and heard their own voice echo back. Both are fixed on macOS, Linux and Windows.
- Emoji suggestions:
  - Every word that matches an emoji lights up as soon as you finish typing it.
  - Right-click a word to get suggestions for it, to stop suggesting emoji for that word, or to turn suggestions off. Settings › Chats › Emoji lists the words you've turned off.
  - `:word` and a word you point at share one bar, as wide as the message box, and it opens into a grid.
  - With Premium, your custom emoji follow the standard ones.
- On Windows, one closed connection could stall every later one, which hung Agent access and launch requests. That no longer happens.

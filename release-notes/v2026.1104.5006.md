Quill is an unofficial Telegram client and still in beta.

### Chats

- Opening a chat with a long run of unread messages puts the "Unread messages" bar at the top of the view, with what you already read above it. When the unread part fits on one screen, or nothing is unread, the chat opens at the bottom.
- The view holds still while older or newer messages load, rows are edited or deleted, and media finishes loading.
- Sending or replying after scrolling up takes you back to the latest message.
- The ↓ button goes to the bottom. Before, with unread messages it kept returning to the unread bar you were already on. Scrolled up above the bar, the first press goes to the bar and the second to the bottom.
- **Mark as read** in a chat's menu now reads the whole chat, its forum topics, mentions and reactions. Before, it could read only the part of the chat that was loaded.
- The reaction heart above the composer goes away once the reacted message is on screen.
- Sending a sticker from the suggestions row clears the emoji that brought it up.

### Window

- The right column (profile, shared media, downloads) slides in and out instead of popping.

### About

- About has a Share Quill button that copies the website link, and a link to the source code.

### Not yet tested on a live account

- The new chat landing, the ↓ button and Mark as read were checked with tests and offline demos.

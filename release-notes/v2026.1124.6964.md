Quill is an unofficial Telegram client and still in beta.

### Bot invites

- If you own a bot, its profile now has **Invite to Bot**. Pick up to ten people from your chats and Quill invites them to try the bot (#1504).
- When someone invites you to a bot, the chat says who invited you. A bar at the top lets you block the bot or report the person who invited you as spam (#1504).
- Bot invite links open the invite box for a bot you own, or the bot's chat otherwise. Their link previews show an "Invite to Bot" button (#1504).

### Videos

- One press on Play now always plays a chat video, even when its download pauses for a moment or the chat was already loading it in the background (#1498).

### Gifts

- Offers to buy a gift with TON, and gifts bought on the TON blockchain, now show as readable messages with their price (#1504). Quill doesn't move TON: answer those offers in an official app.

### Stickers and security

- The sticker renderer (ThorVG 1.1.4) includes fixes for four security advisories in animated sticker files (#1504).

### Faster and lighter

- Windows that draw shapes such as story rings use about 17 MB less memory on macOS, because paths are now drawn without a full-window texture (#1504).
- Opening a profile or the shared media list sends one request for all media counts instead of up to nine (#1504).
- On Linux, Hebrew points, Arabic vowel marks and other combining marks now sit over their letter (#1504).

### Under the hood

- Updated to TDLib 1.8.71, gpui-kit 0.11, FFmpeg 9.1 (with its reworked AAC encoder for video edits and round videos) and 50 Rust crate updates (#1504).

### Not yet tested on a live account

- These changes were checked with tests, CI on all three systems and offline demos on macOS, not on a live account. They were not run on real Windows or Linux hardware.

### Chats in their own windows

- A sticker chat in its own window no longer leaks while the main window is minimized or closed. Memory grew about 25 MB a second, past 1.3 GB in under a minute. It now stays level.
- A round video or GIF in a chat window now plays while the main window is visible. It used to stay stuck on its grey placeholder while Quill reopened the player 57 times a second. On a Mac, CPU drops from 95% to 16%.
- A clip keeps playing when you switch to another Quill window. Before, it froze or dropped to a stutter. It also wakes with its last picture, without a green flash.
- Stickers in a chat window no longer reload or replay from the start each time the main window switches chats.
- A main window fully covered by its own chat window stops animating and gives back about 14 MB of screen buffers. On a 120 Hz Mac it was drawing about 100 frames a second that nobody could see.

### Less CPU for voice notes and music

- Audio at normal speed no longer runs the speed stretcher's search. That search cost about a quarter of a core on a Mac for any voice note, audio file or, on Linux and Windows, video soundtrack, and it always landed on the same answer.
- Opus voice notes take about 55 times less CPU to decode.

### Also fixed

- Animated emoji in the chat list keep their place when you switch chats. They used to restart from the first frame, and now and then flashed a star or a plain emoji.
- A highlighted word's emoji bar stays open on the way to it. It used to swap or close as soon as the pointer crossed another word.
- Sticker suggestions can be closed, with Esc or a new close button, and come back when the text changes.

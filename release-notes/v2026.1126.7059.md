Quill is an unofficial Telegram client and still in beta.

### Glass effects

- With Glass effects on, the Quill window no longer disappears on macOS after you switch to another app and back (#1513).
- **Settings › Appearance › Glass effects** has a tuning panel with 14 sliders, such as bend, frost, blur, sheen and shadow. A live preview shows a slice of a chat with the glass Quill draws there, and Reset puts every slider back to the shipped look (#1511).
- Menus and panels use a thicker frost, so text behind the main menu no longer reads through it (#1512).
- Hover and selection highlights turn into clear glass with an accent tint when glass is on (#1512).

### Rounder corners

- Hover and selection highlights inside menus, lists and pickers follow the curve of the card around them (#1512).
- The folder pill in Floating lines up its icon, name and unread counter with even spacing (#1512).
- In Floating, the "Unread messages" bar is a centred pill like the date chips, and the folder rail and chat list share one card (#1515).
- Floating also rounds the emoji, sticker and GIF panel, dialogs, Settings groups, search fields, the buttons at the bottom of a chat (Join, Start, Unblock) and the selection bar (#1515).
- The "Quill" caption in the Floating title bar is gone (#1515).

### Fixes

- ⌘, (Ctrl+, on Windows and Linux) opens Settings again after you close Settings. Other app-wide shortcuts and menu commands work again in that case too (#1514).

### Not yet tested on a live account

- These changes were checked with tests, CI on all three systems and offline demos on macOS, not on a live account. They were not run on real Windows or Linux graphics hardware.

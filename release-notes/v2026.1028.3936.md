### Animated emoji

- An emoji sent on its own now turns into a large animated sticker, as on other Telegram apps. It plays when you send it and when it scrolls into view, and it keeps your skin tone.
- Tap it to play it again along with its full-screen effect. When you read an emoji someone sent you in a private chat, its effect plays on its own, and they aren't told you tapped it.
- Messages of two or three custom emoji show larger too.

### Smoother, sharper stickers

- Animated stickers are drawn by a new renderer, ThorVG, at your screen's full resolution. The soft edges and light halo are gone.
- Stickers play at up to 120 fps on high-refresh displays, and stay at the sticker's own rate on 60 Hz screens or when power saving is on.
- The sticker panel uses less memory.

### Also new

- One click on a voice note, song, GIF or video starts the download and plays it when it's ready. A second click cancels.
- Music and voice rows in Shared Media play in place instead of jumping to the message.
- The media viewer has a Reply button.
- Link underlines in wrapped text now stay under the words.
- Downloads that Telegram started by itself can be cancelled.
- Fixed a macOS bug where a window brought back from a notification could stop redrawing.
- Agent access: one switch in Settings > Advanced turns it on or off, and there's a copyable setup prompt for agents.

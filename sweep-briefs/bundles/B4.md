## subsystems-6: Starting a call or a share blocks the UI thread on devices, up to 10 s for a macOS screen share
- Severity: P2
- Kind: stall
- Platforms: all; the screen part is worst on macOS (5 s plus 5 s limits), with `Gpu::new` plus D3D device setup on Windows
- Where: `src/connect/calls/mod.rs:133-139` (`refresh_call_devices`, cpal enumeration in `devices.rs:51-70` plus `qc_video_input_devices`), called at `:244`, `:475` and `src/connect/group_calls/mod.rs:95`. `src/calls/engine/tgcalls/mod.rs:124-142` (`StreamPlayer::open` for call sound, also `reopen_call_sound` from `watch_call_sound`). `src/connect/call_capture.rs:168,202-210` (`CpalMic::start`). `src/connect/call_screen.rs:184` to `src/calls/capture/screen_mac.rs:154` (`shareable_content`) and `:207-209` (`startCaptureWithCompletionHandler`, then `recv_timeout(REPLY_TIMEOUT)`, 5 s each).
- Trigger: a call connects, a voice chat is joined, the camera is turned on, a screen share starts, or the sound watch reopens output.
- Effect and size: `memory-sweep-2026-10-29.md` measured an output open on main at 88 to 119 ms first time and 29 to 36 ms on reopen, before the #1253 fix for playback. Calls were not moved by #1253 (`d87a6063c`). Add enumeration and the mic open: a guess of 100 to 300 ms at each call connect, more with Bluetooth while the profile switches, and all of it inside `ingest` on the UI thread. A macOS share start is a guess of 300 ms or more, with a hard limit of 10 s.
- Why it is real: `open_sound`, `capture.open` and `call_screen.open` have no off-thread path. The Linux portal start is already on a thread (`screen_linux.rs:124`), so that platform is clean for the share.
- Fix sketch: follow #1253: open on a worker and hand the result back through a slot the pump reads. The mic and the output start as "opening". Enumerate on a worker, cached, and connect with the cached list so connect never waits. Backend `start` for macOS and Windows goes to a worker, and the pump picks up the capture.
- Proof plan: tests with injected openers that block on a latch: `pump_call_capture` and `pump_call_screen` return at once, and the state shows "opening". Demo: main-thread stall trace around `call_connect` with a slow fake opener.
- Scope: `call_capture.rs`, `call_screen.rs`, `calls/mod.rs`, `tgcalls/mod.rs`, `tgcalls/devices.rs`. Medium risk. Check against the UI-thread lane for overlap.

# Panel close controls and in-chat search bar

Panels and popovers (emoji, GIF, folder, mute and timer menus, privacy, spellcheck, story page, chat search, call invite, instant view) closed with a "Close" text button, while kit dialogs use an ✕ icon. They now use the ✕ icon, with a tooltip and accessible name. Dialog footers keep their text "Close" button; text is standard in a footer next to other actions.

The in-chat search bar is one row: field, "n of m", ▲ older / ▼ newer and ✕. It used to put "Older" / "Newer" text buttons on a second line.
